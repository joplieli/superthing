# Treatment v2 — "THE DAWNWORKS" (working title)

Synthesis of round-1 critique: story engine of pitch D (robot + girl + jammed sunrise) + the twist "a nest in the gears" + material language of pitch A (paper, card, tin, brass) + a world logic of a toy theatre.

## World logic (the conceit)
A miniature hand-made world, shot like a stop-motion film (macro lens, shallow focus, characters animated "on twos", camera moves smooth at 24 fps). It is a theatre-world: the night sky is a black card pricked with pinholes with light behind them; clouds are cotton wool hanging on threads; background townsfolk are painted paper flats sliding in slots; the sun is a big warm lantern on a mechanical arm. Every morning somebody has to crank it up. That somebody is HOB.

## Cast
- HOB — an old lithographed tin robot, the stagehand of dawn. Big hands, two warm eye-lamps, a mechanical day-counter on his chest (41 275). The walls of his tower are covered with thousands of scratched tally marks — one per sunrise, all alone.
- WREN — a 7-year-old girl puppet (felt/knit pyjamas, painted wooden head with a face that is replaced like stop-motion faces), keeps a chalk tally of sunrises on her bedroom wall. Small hands.
- A paper mother bird with a nest of three eggs.
- Paper extras: a rooster, a baker with a lantern, a man with a pocket watch, a cat.

## Beats (~96 s)
1. 0:00 ECU of one glowing pinhole star; pull back: the sky is black card with pinholes, cotton clouds on threads sway. Crane down to a paper hill-town at night. Clock tower: 6:12. Ticking.
2. 0:07 The town is waiting: rooster on a fence opens its beak — nothing. A baker holds a lantern up at the black sky. A man taps his pocket watch. Everyone looks up.
3. 0:13 Wren's room: alarm clock rings into darkness. She sits up, frowns at the black window, at her chalk tally on the wall (no mark for today), at the clock. At the window: up on the hill the silhouette of the Dawnworks, a dim lantern below the horizon, a distant CLANK.
4. 0:20 Inside the Dawnworks: gears, chains, a giant crank. Hob strains, feet slide, steam hisses from his joints, eye-lamps flicker. Counter 41 275. He lets go, shakes his hand, oils the crank, tries again. CLANK. Shoulders drop.
5. 0:32 Wren climbs up the ladder, breath fogging. Hob startles. She grabs the crank and yanks with her whole weight — it gives a millimetre and from inside the machine comes a CHIRP. Both freeze. Look at each other.
6. 0:38 POV from inside the gear housing: their two faces peering in, lit by her lantern. Wedged between the teeth: a paper nest with three eggs and a furious mother bird. Hob reaches in — his fingers are too big, clink; the bird pecks his finger. He looks at Wren. She rolls up her sleeve, reaches in slowly (hand close-up), lifts the nest out and sets it on a safe beam.
7. 0:48 Together on the crank: her small hands beside his big ones. He nods the count — one, two — pull. The music locks to the ratchet rhythm. Gears turn, the chain runs, the great arm swings.
8. 0:58 DAWN: the black pinhole card rolls up like a theatre drop, revealing a painted dawn gradient; the sun-lantern rises over the paper horizon on its arm; light sweeps across the town, shadows stretch and rotate, paper windows glow gold, the rooster finally crows, the baker smiles, street lamps switch off one by one, paper birds lift from the roofs. The clock says 6:26 — late, and nobody minds.
9. 1:12 Back in the tower, warm light. Counter clicks 41 276. One egg cracks; a tiny beak. Wren takes her chalk and adds one mark beside Hob's thousands of scratches. Hob looks at it for a long beat, then pats the beam beside him. She sits, legs dangling; after a hesitation he sits too.
10. 1:22 Pull back: the Dawnworks on the hill, the sun up, the town alive. Line: "Nothing rises alone." End card: "Every frame and every sound of this film was written in code."

## Style
Procedural stop-motion miniature: card, paper fibre, halftone print on the flats, lithographed tin with scratches, brass, felt, chalk, cotton. Practical lighting: blue moon gel + warm window/lantern practicals at night; a hard warm sun key at dawn. Macro DOF, light haze, film grain, slight halation. Score: music box + low pad + strings; the ratchet of the crank is the metronome; SFX all synthesized.
