# Concept pitches (round 1)

Production constraints (hard):
- Everything procedural, written in code: three.js/WebGL shaders + Canvas2D textures. No external images/models/fonts beyond system fonts, no AI image/video generation.
- Software rendering (SwiftShader) on 2 CPUs, 1920x1080, 24 fps, 60-110 s. Audio synthesized in Python (music + SFX). No voice-over, only on-screen text.
- Must have characters with real acting: facial expression, hands/fingers, body mechanics, physics of secondary motion, living environment (air, particles, ambient motion), cinematic camera, light, textures.

Reference to be DIFFERENT from (not copy): OpenAI 77 s film "The Museum of What Could Be": painterly soft-3D look with grain, pastel peach/lavender palette; a woman with round glasses writes words on paper that become reality (a flower blooms, a floating city rises from fog, a valley paints itself green), glowing neon handwriting "A PERFECT FUTURE", swirling flow-field of equations, "PEOPLE BUILDING THEIR OWN FUTURES", a museum of futuristic exhibits on ribbon walkways, end card "There are many futures. Which do you want to build?" + logo.

## A. "UNWRITTEN" — a pop-up book
Look: physical paper craft shot like macro photography. Pop-up book mechanics, real paper fibres, ink halftone print, soft lamp shadows, shallow depth of field, warm tungsten desk lamp vs cool moonlight from a window; dawn at the end.
Cast: PIP, a tiny paper girl printed in the book, a paper marionette with brass split-pin joints; ink-printed face that changes expression.
Beats:
1. Night, a desk. Dust motes in a lamp beam, tea steam, curtain breathing in a draft. An old cloth book, gilt title "TOMORROW".
2. The cover lifts by itself... Pip is pushing it from inside. Peeks out, blinks at the giant room.
3. Spread 1 is flat print of a primitive village. She yanks a "PULL" tab: the village pops up (huts, fire of paper flames, a wheel). Camera drops to her scale.
4. She runs and heaves the page over: a giant page-turn becomes the transition. Spread 2: industrial city pops up (bridges, a train on a paper track, smoke puffs, windows lighting from behind).
5. Page-turn. Spread 3: the space age (rocket on a slider, satellites on wire, moon on a pivot) — she rides the rocket tab.
6. Last spread: blank white. Silence. Pip touches the empty paper. Doubt. Then she starts folding the blank page herself — an origami crane folds itself fold by fold.
7. She rides the crane off the book, across the desk, past the steam, out of the window into the real dawn.
8. Scale reveal: camera pulls back from the window — the house is a pop-up, the city is a pop-up, the horizon is the gutter of a gigantic book whose next page is just turning.
End line: "The best pages haven't been written yet."

## B. "WHY NOT" — a question that travels
Look: 2D gouache night painting, indigo + sodium orange, rain and refraction, bioluminescent glows.
Cast: a 7-year-old girl who can't sleep; city night workers.
Beats: 3 am, rain on window. She fogs the glass with breath, finger-writes "why?". The letters glow, drip, become a firefly-spark that flies into the city. One continuous camera ride following the spark over rooftops, through a lab window (a tired scientist looks up), a bakery, an observatory. The spark collects other people's questions ("how?", "what if?") — a swarm that rises and arranges itself into a constellation; dawn. Back at the window: her "why?" has been completed from outside into "why not?".
End line: "Every future starts with a question."

## C. "RELAY" — hands passing a light across 10,000 years
Look: stylised clay-like soft 3D, macro on hands, dark backgrounds with the light as the only key.
Beats: one unbroken camera move along a chain of hands handing over a light: cave hand with an ember -> potter -> scribe with an oil lamp -> printer with a candle -> inventor with a bulb -> engineer with a screen glow -> astronaut glove -> a child's hand meets a robot hand; together they let the light go up into a sky that fills with lights.
End line: "Your turn."

## D. "LUMEN" — the robot who raises the dawn
Look: painterly 2.5D storybook, night-blue to gold.
Cast: Lumen, an old lamplighter robot; a girl in pyjamas.
Beats: a hill town under a giant dormant machine. Every night Lumen climbs it to crank the sun up. Tonight the crank jams; he struggles; the girl sees from her window, runs out, and together they turn it. The sun is hoisted on mechanical arms, dawn floods the streets, lamps go out one by one, birds lift.
End line: "Tomorrow doesn't rise by itself."
